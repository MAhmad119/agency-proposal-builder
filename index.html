User Guide

1. Add your agency and client details.

2. Enter the project scope, objectives and deliverables.

3. Add milestones, dates and pricing items.

4. Choose Modern, Minimal or Corporate template.

5. Check the live preview and select Export PDF.

6. In the print dialog, choose Save as PDF.

Tip: Always review the document before sending it to a client.
