User Guide

Learn how to create and export a proposal.

How to create a proposal

  • Choose a proposal design template.
  • Enter your agency and client details.
  • Write a clear project introduction, objectives and scope.
  • Add deliverables and project milestones.
  • Enter pricing, discount, tax and payment schedule.
  • Review terms, revisions and additional notes.
  • Check the live preview for accuracy.
  • Use Print / Save PDF to export your document.

How PDF export works

Click Print / Save PDF. Your browser's print dialog will open. Select "Save as PDF" as the destination and save the document to your device. The exact options depend on your browser and operating system.

Draft saving

Use Save Draft to store your current proposal in this browser. Drafts are stored locally in the browser's local storage. Clearing browser data or using a different device may remove or make the draft unavailable.
